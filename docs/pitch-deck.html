InScene

The ad is in the scene.

AI-rendered product placement that adapts to every viewer, built on Google Cloud with Gemini.

Introduce the team and the one-liner: InScene renders a different, relevant, local ad into the scene for every viewer, with no ad breaks, while the director stays in control.

Problem statement

Video ads interrupt viewers, ignore context and can’t prove they were seen

They interrupt

Pre-roll and mid-roll breaks stop the story. Viewers skip, block, or pay to avoid them.

They ignore context

Product placement is shot once and shown to everyone, in every country and language, forever.

They can’t prove impact

Brands can’t see how visible a placement was, who saw it, or whether it fit the scene.

InScene · 02

Three groups lose: viewers get interrupted, creators leave money on the table because their scenes are sold once, and brands can’t target or measure placements.

InScene · 03

The story, actors and camera never change. Only the surfaces the director approved are re-rendered, and if a render isn’t ready or fails a check, the original shot plays.

What makes it different

Four things generic AI ad insertion doesn’t do

The director decides

Approved surfaces only, a size cap, blocked categories and final sign-off.

The story never breaks

No ad breaks, and the original shot plays whenever a render isn’t ready.

Every ad explains itself

“Why this ad?” shows the reasons, using only city, language and plan.

One scene, sold again

New season, new market, new brand: the same shot earns again, no reshoot.

InScene · 04

InScene · 07

Everything runs in one container on Cloud Run with ffmpeg included. The ad engine is pure TypeScript and unit-tested. Gemini does all the seeing, editing and speaking. Storage is a JSON store today; Firestore, Cloud Storage and BigQuery are the scale-out path.
